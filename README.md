# TileMate

TileMate is a UK-focused tiling job management, measurement, materials, quoting and invoicing application.

## Included in v2

- Job and customer management
- Wall/floor measurement and opening deductions
- Tile, adhesive, grout, silicone, primer, leveller and tanking calculations
- Labour, VAT, deposits and quoting
- Quote/booking/invoice documents
- Site photos and notes
- Business profile studio with public profile, sharing and QR code
- 28-day update centre for dated UK reference information

## Important pricing rule

Online reference updates must never silently overwrite a user's own commercial rates. User-entered labour and merchant prices remain under the user's control.
