# Current UK reference snapshot used for TileMate's v2 update centre

Checked 2 September 2026.

- GOV.UK VAT rates: standard VAT rate 20%.
  https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates
- GOV.UK National Minimum Wage: from April 2026, £12.71/hour for workers aged 21+; this is a statutory floor, not a recommended tiler rate.
  https://www.gov.uk/national-minimum-wage-rates
- ONS Construction Output Price Indices: latest published dataset covers UK construction output price indices through June 2026; the dataset is revised on a scheduled basis.
  https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/constructionindustry/datasets/interimconstructionoutputpriceindices/current
- HSE construction guidance: current construction health and safety guidance and CDM 2015 resources.
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/index.htm
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/
- BSI BS 5385-1:2018 is current for internal wall tiling in normal conditions.
  https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/wall-and-floor-tiling-design-and-installation-of-ceramic-natural-stone-and-mosaic-wall-tiling-in-normal-internal-conditions-code-of-practice
- BSI BS 5385-3:2024 is current for internal/external floor tiling in normal conditions.
  https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/wall-and-floor-tiling-design-and-installation-of-internal-and-external-ceramic-and-mosaic-natural-stone-and-terrazzo-flooring-and-internal-agglomerated-stone-flooring-in-normal-conditions-code-of-practice
- BSI BS 5385-4:2015 is current for tiling in specific conditions including wet areas.
  https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/wall-and-floor-tiling-design-and-installation-of-ceramic-and-mosaic-tiling-in-specific-conditions-code-of-practice
